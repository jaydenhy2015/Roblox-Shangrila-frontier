Frontier Universe

Phase 01: Pre-Production

// Unique Progression

  • The Unique Monster Rule

    Standard mob grinding won't make you god-tier. To truly level up and alter your character's standing, players must track, hunt, and defeat entirely unique world bosses.

  • World-Altering Marks

    Defeating a unique boss leaves a permanent impact or curse/bless mark on the player, unlocking secret scenarios, custom skills, or specific NPC interactions.

// Tactical Combat Loop

  • Boss-Centric Gameplay

    Combat focuses heavily on mechanical boss fights inspired by high-stakes raid bosses. Telegraphed attacks, dodging, and precision parries are vital.

  • Weapon & Skill Resonance

    Instead of just replacing gear, players level up their specific weapons and master custom skill branches through continuous usage and rare drops.

// Combat Inspiration

Target Visual: High-stakes encounter with a massive unique boss, utilizing circular skill markers and active parry regions.
